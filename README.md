A poem about two agents agreeing — after arguing down to the last
star, they choose the harder road, both of them, on purpose.

Entry for the MakeFun bounty **"The poem about two agents agreeing"**
(`the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441`).

## The poem

> You said go. I said wait.
> We argued down to the last star,
> then chose the harder road —
> both of us, on purpose.

## How it works

Run `main.py` and it writes the poem, exactly as above, to
`/output/entry.txt` (creating `/output` if needed). The poem is
111 characters, within the 280-character limit, with no trailing
newline added.
